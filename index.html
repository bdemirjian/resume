ABOUT

I focus on turning ambiguous business problems into clear product direction, measurable outcomes, and executable plans. I am most effective where customer needs, financial and commercial value, data, technical constraints, and organizational priorities intersect. I use evidence to determine what deserves investment, translate objectives into work teams can execute, and make dependencies and tradeoffs visible before they become delivery problems. I draw from product strategy, systems thinking, analytics, software delivery, and customer behavior to frame problems, test assumptions, and identify the highest-leverage opportunities. My approach is pragmatic: clarify the value at stake, establish how we will know if we are right, align stakeholders around the decisions that matter, and use results to determine what comes next.
